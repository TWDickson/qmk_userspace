Seven themes, five of them ours

Every look below is drawn on the board's real LED map — the 70 coordinates and flags out of info.json, with the charcoal plate, opaque greige caps and translucent amber knobs the photographs showed. Four stock-effect themes were cut and four hand-written renderers took their place; what shipped is at the top, what went is below it. Pick one to see it lit, and turn the knobs — hue, saturation and speed are live on all of them now, and they persist.

Where it landed

    
    
      
  • Five of the seven are drawn in the indicator hook, on a SOLID_COLOR
        base coat. ALPHAS_MODS is the one stock effect kept, because
        LED_FLAG_MODIFIER here is exactly the outer pinky column plus the four
        thumbs — the eight keys a half that carry the red Escape, the blue LWR and the peach RSE.
        Its geometry is a fact about the hardware, not about the coordinate system.
    • 
      
  • The other five stock animations are #undef'd in the keymap's
        config.h, which needs no post_config.h because the generated
        info_config.h is included first. A
        _Static_assert(RGB_MATRIX_EFFECT_MAX == 3) guards it, since undefining
        someone else's define otherwise fails silently.
    • 
      
  • Net −152 bytes, four new renderers included. Dropping the stock effects
        paid for them.
    • 
      
  • The knobs are live and now persist. Every renderer reads
        rgb_matrix_get_hue() and friends rather than the theme table, so a theme is a
        starting point rather than a cage — and THEME_NEXT writes it to EEPROM, so
        your adjusted version is what comes back after a reboot.
    • 
      
  • Holding both thumbs swaps the starfield for a config readout — theme name, four
        knob bars, MAC/WIN and BASE/GAME. Bars rather than numbers because the OLED font is A-Z
        with no digits, and draw_text() indexes it with c - 'A'.
